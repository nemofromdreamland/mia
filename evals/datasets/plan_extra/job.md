Rota Azul Logística

Junior Data Engineer

About the company
Rota Azul is a logistics company with 800 people, based in Recife, PE. Our data team has eight people and supports routing, delivery tracking and customer service. We work in a hybrid model.

About the role
We are looking for a junior data engineer to help build and maintain the pipelines that feed our analytics. You will work with a senior engineer who will review your work in the first months.

Responsibilities
Build and maintain batch pipelines in Python and SQL.
Orchestrate pipelines with Airflow, including retries and alerts.
Write data quality tests and investigate failed checks.
Document each pipeline so the whole team can maintain it.
Talk to analysts to understand which tables they need.

Requirements
Good knowledge of Python and SQL.
Experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL.
Git in the daily routine.
Willingness to learn and to ask for help.
Clear communication with technical and non-technical people.

Nice to have
Experience with Airflow or a similar orchestrator.
Basic knowledge of Docker.
Notions of data modeling.

We offer
Competitive salary, meal allowance, health plan and a yearly training budget.
